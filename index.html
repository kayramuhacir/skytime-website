The difference

Not a flight tracker. Not an entertainment app.

A tracker tells you where the plane is. SkyTime tells you what to do with the time inside it — a personal planning layer for the hours between takeoff and landing, online or offline.

Questions

Does it work without wifi?

Yes. Build your plan on the ground and it runs offline for the whole flight. No connection needed in the air.

Is it a flight tracker?

No. SkyTime doesn't track the plane — it plans your time. It's a planning layer, not a map.

Which flights does it work for?

Any flight, though it earns its place on long-haul. The longer the block of time, the more there is to plan.

What does it cost?

SkyTime is a paid app, with a free trial so you can plan your first flight before you commit. Pricing is shown at launch.
