SkyTime turns a long flight into a structured block of time instead of a dead zone. Before departure, it builds a plan for the flight using how many hours are in the air, so the plan matches the actual time available — not a generic to-do list.

The plan works offline. Once content or tasks are loaded before boarding, no connection is needed during the flight. This matters because most long-haul flights have weak, expensive, or no wifi.

SkyTime is meant for the person who wants to land having actually made progress — on work, on a personal project, on rest — rather than landing having just scrolled through the flight.

SkyTime is a planning tool, not a flight-status tracker (it doesn't show gate numbers or delays) and not an entertainment app (it doesn't host movies or games itself). It sits alongside whatever content or work you bring, and organizes your time around it.
