Is SkyTime available now? Not yet — SkyTime is pre-launch. Join the waitlist on the website and you'll be emailed when it's ready.

Who is SkyTime for? Long-haul travelers, roughly 20 to 32 years old — digital nomads, young professionals, anyone who flies long distances and wants to use that time well.

Does SkyTime need wifi? No. Plans are built before boarding and work fully offline during the flight.

Is SkyTime a flight tracker? No. It doesn't track gates, delays, or flight status. It's a planning layer for your time in the air.

Who built SkyTime? Kiana, the founder, built SkyTime alone to solve what she calls one of the most overlooked problems in travel: wasted time at 35,000 feet.
