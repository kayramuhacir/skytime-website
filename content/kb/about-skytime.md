SkyTime is a personal planning layer for the time between takeoff and landing. It is not a flight tracker and not entertainment. It helps you use long-haul flight time well, whether you have wifi or not.

SkyTime's tagline is "Your time. Your altitude."

SkyTime was founded by Kiana. She is the sole founder, building for long-haul travelers aged roughly 20 to 32 — digital nomads and young professionals who don't want to waste hours at 35,000 feet.

The core idea: before you board, SkyTime helps you build a plan for your flight — what to work on, read, watch, or rest through — broken into the actual hours you'll have in the air. It works offline, since most long-haul flights have limited or no connectivity.

SkyTime is currently pre-launch. People can join the waitlist on the website to be notified when the app is available.
